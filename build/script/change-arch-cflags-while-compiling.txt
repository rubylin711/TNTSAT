how to modify architecture compile option for individual makefile or script

note: -mthumb must be followed a space to distinguish with -mthumb-interwork

modify CC in Makefile
env.mk
CC := arm-linux-gnueabihf-gcc -march=armv7-a -mtune=cortex-a7 -mabi=aapcs-linux -mfloat-abi=hard -mfpu=neon-vfpv4 -mthumb -mthumb-interwork
some specific Makefile
CC := $(subst -mthumb ,,$(CC))

modify CC in shell script
export CC="arm-linux-gnueabihf-gcc -march=armv7-a -mtune=cortex-a7 -mabi=aapcs-linux -mfloat-abi=hard -mfpu=neon-vfpv4 -mthumb -mthumb-interwork"
CC_BAK=${CC}
CCC="`echo ${CC/-mthumb /}`"
CC=${CCC}
........
CC=${CC_BAK}
